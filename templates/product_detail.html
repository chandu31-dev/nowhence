Chest

Lay a T-shirt you like flat and measure straight across, just below the underarms. Double that number to compare with the garment chest measurement.

Shoulder

Measure across the back from one shoulder seam to the other, following the seam line rather than the curve of your body.

Body Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder beside the collar to the bottom hem of the T-shirt.

Sleeve

Measure from the shoulder seam to the sleeve opening along the outside edge.

Choose Your Fit

Use the classic chart for a clean everyday fit and the oversized chart for a roomier silhouette. Compare garment measurements, not body measurements.

Pro Tips from our Master Tailor

  • Lay the garment on a flat surface and smooth it without stretching the fabric.
  • Use a soft measuring tape and keep it straight along seams.
  • Measure twice for accuracy before placing your order.
  • If you prefer a closer fit, compare chest measurements with a tee you already own.
  • For a relaxed silhouette, choose the oversized fit chart.
  • Allow for small variations between styles and fabric construction.
